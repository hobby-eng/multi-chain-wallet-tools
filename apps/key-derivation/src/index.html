Wallet Key Derivation Tool

Derive standard Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Dash wallet keys from a 12- or 24-word English BIP39 recovery phrase.

!
Use an offline, trusted computer. Anyone who sees your recovery phrase or private keys can control those funds. Test with an empty wallet first. This app does not save or send your input, but a compromised browser or operating system can still steal secrets.
