Recognising the wallet

A fingerprint has 32 bits: it is a filter, not proof, since a wrong candidate passes about once in four billion. It is the fingerprint of the wallet itself: with Seedshift, of the phrase before the shift, which Encode shows as the original fingerprint. The check unmasks each candidate with the dates first, so the encoded fingerprint never matches.

An address compares the wallet itself. It names its coin: it is read as every coin whose form it has and looked for on the paths that each coin's wallets use, a path two coins share once, at the account, branch and index below, by default the first receiving address of account 0, among as many addresses from there as the number says.

A Bitcoin address's type names its path. A ypub or upub is looked for at BIP49 and a zpub or vpub at BIP84; another extended key, a public key or a WIF at BIP44, BIP49, BIP84 and BIP86. An account xpub is compared at its account.

A WIF can spend the funds. It stays in this offline page and is compared in the search worker only.
