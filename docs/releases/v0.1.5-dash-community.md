## Safety notes

Use the Deriver and Inspector offline for sensitive material. Connected lookups reveal queried public identifiers and source IP addresses to providers. Orchard viewing keys reveal wallet activity even though they cannot spend. Recovery records and private exports must be protected according to their threshold/access design. Restore and move valuable funds with a maintained Dash wallet after independently confirming the displayed paths, keys, addresses, and balances.
