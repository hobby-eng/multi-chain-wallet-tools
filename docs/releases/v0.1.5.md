- Added the Photo or MP3 tab, MnemoCode's steganography: a message, a small file or a MnemoCode backup record hidden in a JPEG photograph or an MP3 file under a container password, optionally with a decoy under a second password, or divided across 2 to 16 files of which a quorum recovers it. The guaranteed space at every density is shown first; every new file is read back from the Blob saved and opened with each password before it is offered. Encode's records and a pattern's legend line can be hidden there, and what it opens goes on to Decode or Re-encode. It is not statistical invisibility; recompression or resizing destroys the record.
- Added MnemoCode's QR codes with a hidden record: a card sheet's QR can show the printed contact to ordinary scanners and keep the record in its hidden bytes, and every MnemoCode QR image import reads such a record first. It is camouflage, not encryption.

- Added MHFE's decrypt with several passwords: Decode asks every password, with its own PIM and check-word repair, and for a 24-word container the original seed phrase's BIP39 passphrase for the 16-bit check, before any work, then recovers each password in turn and shows every seed phrase on one screen under its password.

The tables are generated from Unicode's `UnicodeData.txt` and checked against all of its `NormalizationTest.txt`.
- Added an explicit MHFE wallet-check checkbox: the Deriver's own generator samples phrases, on up to four workers with WebCrypto's PBKDF2, until the first two bytes of the check derived from the complete BIP39 seed are zero, and MHFE's wallet module checks the phrase once more before it is shown; it uses the phrase and the nonempty BIP39 passphrase of the one passphrase field, which asks you to check it with Show. Generation can be stopped and shows its draws, its time and an average-time estimate. The phrase and seed bytes are not overwritten. Hover or click the adjacent “?” to read the explanation.

The Scanner checks a seed phrase's addresses on each coin's wallet paths, the addresses the Deriver derives (Solana's Phantom and Ledger paths, Stellar's SEP-0005 accounts, NEAR's implicit accounts, and for XRP and Tron the BIP44 address index and Ledger Live's account index), each through its own 20-address gap; the Viewer shows an address's balance, and its sequence for XRP and Stellar; NEAR's Viewer takes the account's public key (`ed25519:…`), since 64 hex digits cannot be told from a private key. Tokens, staking and history are not read, and these coins have no custom paths or public-key scans. - Added Cosmos, Injective, Hedera and TON to the Discovery Scanner and the Activity Viewer as account coins, mainnet only, under "Other networks". The Scanner checks Cosmos's and Injective's BIP44 address index and Ledger Live's account index, Hedera's Ed25519 and ECDSA keys, and TON's Trust Wallet and Ledger keys as v4R2 wallets. ## Safety notes

Use the Deriver and Inspector offline for secrets. Connected Viewer and Scanner requests disclose public identifiers and the source IP address to configured providers. Recovery shares, envelopes, private descriptors, private keys, and restored seed material all grant or contribute to spending access and must be protected according to their threshold design. None of the tools broadcasts a transaction or replaces independent verification in a maintained standard wallet. MHFE is experimental and not independently reviewed.
