## Safety notes

Use the Deriver and Inspector offline for secrets. Connected Viewer and Scanner requests disclose public identifiers and the source IP address to configured providers. Recovery shares, envelopes, private descriptors, private keys, and restored seed material all grant or contribute to spending access and must be protected according to their threshold design. None of the tools broadcasts a transaction or replaces independent verification in a maintained standard wallet.
