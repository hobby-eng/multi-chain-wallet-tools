## Review evidence

### Scope and methodology

Ordinary audit with a compact team (AGENTS.md): one coordinator and four reviewers with distinct scopes, not the expanded specialist workflow. The coordinator ran the baseline checks once on the reviewed tree and shared their logs; the reviewers wrote audit-only harnesses in docs/audits/AUD-023-harnesses/ and recorded every command they relied on. The coordinator challenged each candidate against the source before accepting it (21 accepted; several candidates were merged or kept as observations). The owner then authorised the remediation in the same working tree and decided four questions: a selective build offers MnemoCode's address check for its own coins only; two more release runners; JSON documents under format:check; MnemoCode's share-restore orchestration waits for its next round. During remediation MnemoCode 7dd510b648015a4d553b5be24ab2564f6edbb6b2 and MHFE 0.5.1 were vendored again, which brought Decode with marked codes, the heir-sheet known answers and MHFE's quick and full class checks into the Deriver. The final checks ran once on the remediation tree; harnesses whose expectations described the reviewed snapshot were adapted and say so in their files and READMEs. On 2026-10-08 the owner limited checks before a release to about two minutes each: the full unit suite, the build, the artifact checks, the Chromium browser regressions and the reviewers' probes ran; the Firefox regressions, selective builds and the launcher checks wait for the release and are listed as not run. Public test data only: 'abandon ... about', published vectors, the private key 1, synthetic lists.

Included:

- The AUD-022 remediation (twenty findings) in the four tools and the Dash release workflow
- Startup and full self-tests added to the Deriver, PSBT Inspector, Discovery Scanner and Activity Viewer, in both editions
- MnemoCode adoption (c7a45234f4dac0935172fd0ede9d6c5d341524b2 at baseline, 7dd510b648015a4d553b5be24ab2564f6edbb6b2 at the end): recovery panels, workers, cards, shares, wallet recognition and parity with MnemoCode's command line

- Build composition of selective Deriver builds, provenance and release gates, release workflows and documentation
