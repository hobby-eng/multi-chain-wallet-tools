### Findings

#### AUD-019-SEC001 — Medium — The public-input guard lets separator-joined and unseparated recovery phrases through as DPNS names

- **Category:** SEC.
- **Severity:** medium.
- **Status:** verified.
- **Release blocking:** false.
- **Affected files and builds:** `packages/secret-boundary/src/public-input-guard.ts`; Activity Viewer and watch-only Discovery Scanner, both editions.
- **Reproduction:** Enter the public test phrase `zoo … wrong` joined by hyphens, written without separators, or one word per line with a wrong last word, in single or batch input (`public-input-guard.audit.test.ts`).
- **Expected behavior:** Any input that is a recovery phrase in a recognisable form is refused locally before any provider is chosen.
- **Observed behavior:** Three of 25 forms were accepted as DPNS identity names. Spaces, case, line breaks, commas, dots and underscores were refused.
- **Impact:** The Identity lookup would have sent the phrase to the Platform provider. About 37.8 % of random 12-word phrases written without spaces, and 0.18 % joined by hyphens, fit a DPNS label (`dpns-length-fraction`, local). Prerequisite: the person types their own phrase into a public-data tool in one of these forms. No request was made by the probe.
- **Evidence:** Harness 3 of 25 failed at the reviewed commit, 25 of 25 passed after the fix; `packages/secret-boundary/tests/public-input-guard.test.ts`.
- **Recommended fix:** Split at every separator and also try cutting unseparated text into BIP39 words; refuse twelve words of one list in a row in a batch regardless of the checksum.
- **Required verification:** All 26 forms are refused in single and batch input; ordinary DPNS names still pass.

#### AUD-019-API001 — Low — Chosen-words generation accepts a random source that returns too few bytes

- **Category:** API.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Status:** verified.
- **Release blocking:** false.
- **Affected files and builds:** `packages/crypto-core/src/bip39-chosen-words.ts`.
- **Reproduction:** Inject a random source that returns one byte fewer than requested and generate 2,000 phrases (`chosen-words.audit.test.ts`).
- **Expected behavior:** A source of the wrong length or type is refused, as the SLIP-39 share generator does.
- **Observed behavior:** 3 of 2,000 phrases had an invalid BIP39 checksum.
- **Impact:** Not reachable from the page, which always uses `crypto.getRandomValues`; the option exists for tests and the startup self-test. The output distribution with a correct source passed the chi-square probes.
- **Evidence:** Harness 1 of 4 failed at the reviewed commit; after the fix 2,000 of 2,000 short-source calls are refused (`chosen-words-probe-fixed.json`, local); `bip39-chosen-words.test.ts`.
- **Recommended fix:** Require a `Uint8Array` of exactly the requested length; wipe and refuse anything else.
- **Required verification:** Short and wrong-type sources are refused; valid sources give the same phrases as before.

#### AUD-019-API002 — Low — SSKR encoding check accepts Object.prototype names

- **Category:** API.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Status:** verified.
- **Release blocking:** false.
- **Related findings:** AUD-017-API001.
- **Affected files and builds:** `packages/recovery-backup/src/sskr.ts`.
- **Reproduction:** Call share creation or recovery with the encoding `toString`, `constructor`, `__proto__` or `hasOwnProperty`.
- **Expected behavior:** Only the two supported encodings are accepted (the contract restored for AUD-017-API001).
